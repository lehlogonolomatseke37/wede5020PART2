FAQ

Frequently
asked questions.

How does NeighbourlySpace work?

Send us a message on WhatsApp with the service you need, and we'll connect you with a trusted local professional who serves your area.

Is the app available to download?

Not yet — our app is currently in development. Until it launches, every request is handled personally through WhatsApp.

What areas do you serve?

We're starting close to home across Alberton — including Meyersdal, Brackenhurst, New Redruth, Randhart and Glenvista — before expanding across Gauteng.

How quickly will I get a response?

We aim to respond as quickly as possible during business hours, usually within the same day.

Are the professionals verified?

Yes — every professional we recommend is vetted before being added to our network of trusted local providers.

How do I make a booking?

Once we connect you with a professional, you can agree on a time directly with them and confirm your booking through WhatsApp.
