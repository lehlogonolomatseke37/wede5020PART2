About Us

Simpler, safer, more connected communities.

NeighbourlySpace was built to make life inside gated communities simpler, safer and more connected — starting with the estates we call home in Alberton.

Our Story

Built to bridge a gap
inside our own estates.

We saw residents struggling to find reliable service providers, and no simple way to stay informed and connected within their own estate. NeighbourlySpace was built to bridge that gap.

Our Vision

The most trusted community platform in South Africa.
